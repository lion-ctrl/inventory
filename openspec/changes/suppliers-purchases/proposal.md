## Why

The POS records everything that leaves the store and nothing that enters it. Stock only ever goes **down** — `sales.checkout` decrements it, `sales.syncOffline` decrements it — or sideways through a manual `products.adjustStock`. There is no record of **who supplies the store**, what was ordered, or what was paid for it, so the owner reconciles purchases on paper and then re-types the resulting quantities into the catalog by hand.

Two apps were used as reference. From **Inventario**, the supplier form (BASICS / COMMUNICATION / COMPANY ADDRESS / PAYMENT / META); from **Treinta**, only the two supplier-related capabilities its plan advertises: *"Creación de proveedores"* and the expense half of *"Registro de ventas y gastos"*. Half of Inventario's fields do not survive contact with Venezuela — IBAN and BIC are SEPA banking, `VAT ID` is the **RIF** (a pair the app already models as `taxPrefix` + `taxId` on `clients`), and a split `ZIP / City / Country (ISO)` address only pays off for importers, where `clients` stores one flat `address`.

The owner scoped the money deliberately and twice: a "gasto" is **only** a purchase from a supplier (no rent, salaries or utilities), the product keeps **only** its selling price (no cost field), and a purchase carries **one global amount** for the whole order rather than a per-unit cost. That trade is accepted with its consequence stated: the system will report **what was spent per supplier**, and cannot report **profit or margin per product**.

## What Changes

- **NEW `suppliers` table (`convex/schema.ts`)** — a party record deliberately shaped as the mirror of `clientFields`: the same `taxPrefix` + `taxId` RIF pair, the same flat `address`, the same optional-contact shape. Supplier-only additions: `contactName` (a company answers through a person), `mobile` kept apart from `phone` (WhatsApp is the working channel), `paymentTerms` as free text (Venezuelan terms are negotiated per deal, not picked from a list), `website`, `notes`, and `active`. Indexed `by_taxId`.
- **NEW `convex/suppliers.ts`** — `list` / `create` / `update` / `remove`. The read/write split copies `clients`: reading the base is OPERATIONAL (session-gated, because the purchase form must pick one), editing it is MANAGEMENT (`manage_suppliers`). A duplicate RIF is refused through the index — the same supplier entered twice would split its purchase history in two.
- **NEW `manage_suppliers` permission** — added to `permissionsValidator` (`convex/schema.ts`), `PermissionId` (`convex/permissions.ts`), and the `PERMISSIONS` catalog (`src/lib/rbac.ts`), positioned next to `manage_clients`. Declared `v.optional(v.boolean())` so pre-existing employee documents stay schema-valid; an absent key reads as `false` through `can()` — fail-closed.
- **NEW `purchases` table + `convex/purchases.ts`** — a supplier order. Lines carry `productId` + frozen `name` + `qty` and **no unit cost**; the money lives once, as the order's global `total`. `purchases.create` is the **only write in the app that raises stock**; `purchases.remove` is its inverse. Indexed `by_supplier`.
- **Money contract identical to a payment split** — `total` in USD is canonical, `entered` + `currency` preserve what the owner actually typed, and `exchangeRate` freezes the Bs rate of that day (the same lesson `sales.syncOffline` already learned about `ivaPct`/`exchangeRate`). The USD total is **derived server-side** from `entered` + `currency` + `settings.bsRate`, so a client can never submit a total that disagrees with what was typed.
- **NEW Suppliers screen (`src/screens/Suppliers.tsx`, route `/proveedores`)** — a structural twin of `Clients.tsx`: list, search, status filter, pager, detail sheet, create/edit sheet, delete confirmation. The purchase history and the "Registrar compra" form live inside the supplier's detail, not on a screen of their own.
- **Offline, per the existing per-screen contract** — the supplier list is Dexie-mirrored (`db.version(3)`, `src/state/db.ts`) and readable offline; every supplier write is blocked offline with the standard notice; the mirror is cleared on logout alongside the others. Purchases are **online-only** and deliberately unmirrored: they move stock, and offline the history says so instead of rendering an empty list that would read as "you never ordered anything".

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `supplier-registry`: the store keeps a base of the companies it buys from, identified by a unique RIF, gated by a dedicated `manage_suppliers` permission, readable offline from the mirror and unwritable offline.
- `supplier-purchases`: a purchase from a supplier records which products arrived and in what quantity, raises stock by those quantities, and carries one global amount whose USD value is derived server-side against a frozen exchange rate; deleting it returns the stock.

## Impact

- **Backend / convex** — new `convex/suppliers.ts` and `convex/purchases.ts`; `convex/schema.ts` gains `supplierFields`, `purchaseItemValidator`, `purchaseCurrencyValidator`, `purchaseFields`, their doc validators, the `suppliers` (`by_taxId`) and `purchases` (`by_supplier`) tables, and `manage_suppliers` inside `permissionsValidator`; `convex/permissions.ts` widens `PermissionId`.
- **Client / src** — new `src/screens/Suppliers.tsx`; `src/AppShell.tsx` gains the `/proveedores` route, its `ROUTE_IDS` entry and a Nav item behind `<Guard perm="manage_suppliers">`; `src/state/db.ts` `db.version(3)` adds the `suppliers` store; `src/state/hooks.ts` adds `useSuppliers`; `src/state/useMirroredQuery.ts` widens `MirrorTable`; `src/state/SessionContext.tsx` clears the new mirror on logout; `src/types.ts` adds `Supplier` and `Purchase`; `src/lib/rbac.ts` adds the permission, replaces three `Object.fromEntries(...) as PermissionMap` casts with an explicit `perms()` builder, and exports `ALL_PERMISSION_IDS`; `src/components/Icon.tsx` registers `truck`.
- **Design fidelity** — `designs/` has no `suppliers.jsx` and `src/styles/` is a verbatim CSS port that must not gain classes, so the screen is built **entirely from classes Clients and Sale already use** (`lrow`, `prod-detail-*`, `client-field`, `taxid-row`, `cartrow`, `qty`, `pager-*`, `card`). No CSS is added.
- **Dependencies** — none.
- **Tests** — `tests/convex/suppliers.test.ts` (CRUD, permission split, duplicate RIF, empty-string clearing, deactivation); `tests/convex/purchases.test.ts` (stock raised, name/rate freezing, Bs→USD conversion, rejected inputs, all-or-nothing rollback, per-supplier scoping, stock reversal clamped at zero); `tests/components/suppliers-offline-blocking.test.tsx` (offline write-blocking, status filter, the two mirrored amount fields). `tests/convex/fixtures.ts` gains an `admin` employee holding granular permissions (never `'all'`), so a supervision test cannot pass by accident.
- **Data** — additive. `manage_suppliers` is optional, so existing employee documents remain valid; the operational consequence is that **only the owner (`permissions: 'all'`) can manage suppliers until the permission is ticked** for each employee. No migration, no backfill.
- **Out of scope (this change)** — per-unit cost, cost of goods sold, and therefore profit or margin per product; operating expenses (rent, salaries, utilities); purchase orders, receiving against an order, and accounts payable; a printable purchase voucher ("comprobante"); offline purchases; and any profit-and-loss screen. The purchase line shape leaves room for a later `unitCost` without moving anything else.
